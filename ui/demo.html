“The LLM interprets; code decides.”

The design principle behind the pipeline — nothing a business must audit is left to a model.

01 · Ingest

One webhook, any channel

Email, web form, or portal — everything lands on POST /webhook/arcvault-intake and gets a normalized ID, source, and timestamp.

02 · Interpret

A local LLM reads the message

llama3.1:8b (Ollama) classifies, extracts identifiers, and scores its own confidence. Output is schema-constrained — the model physically cannot emit malformed JSON.

03 · Decide

Plain rules make the call

A routing table, a 0.70 confidence gate, and five escalation rules — auditable JavaScript a business can change without retraining anything.

04 · Act

The right team hears about it

The owning team is emailed, a structured JSON record is persisted per request, and the caller gets the full record back.
